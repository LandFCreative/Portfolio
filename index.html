Hello.

Welcome to my interior design portfolio.

I am an Interior Designer with a passion for creating environments that support human wellbeing, creativity, and connection. Drawing from a diverse background in healthcare, design, and community engagement, I approach each project with curiosity, empathy, and a commitment to understanding the people who will use the space. I have a specialized interest in furniture and lighting design, and a significant experience background in industrial design and healthcare design.

My ultimate goal is to find a place that brings out my strengths and strengthens my weaknesses; an environment I can thrive in and grow.

On a personal level, I grew up in Canada, and have been travelling the world since I was young enough to go. First in the RV with my family- hitting 41 of the 50 American states. Then my travels took me to over 45 countries and counting. When home- I love being outside with my dog in any form - camping, hiking, lake time, gardening, paddle boarding, checking out architecture and more.

It would be an honour to work with you. Hope to hear from you soon.

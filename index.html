Hello.

Welcome to my interior design portfolio.

I am an emerging Interior Designer with a passion for creating environments that support human wellbeing, creativity, and connection. Drawing from a diverse background in healthcare, design, and community engagement, I approach each project with curiosity, empathy, and a commitment to understanding the people who will use the space.
My design philosophy is grounded in human-centered design, accessibility, and evidence-based decision making. I believe that successful environments balance function and beauty while fostering inclusivity, adaptability, and a strong sense of place. Through thoughtful planning, material selection, and attention to user experience, I strive to create spaces that are both meaningful and memorable.
